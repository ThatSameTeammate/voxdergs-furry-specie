Voxdergs

Voxdergs are kind and fluffy anthropomorphic dragons fused with outdoor warning sirens. Their voices warn the people of two cities about approaching storms.

They live in a world that survived global disasters. Humans and dragons built a new order together, and at its center are the Voxdergs — not as soldiers or superheroes, but as a reliable part of the rescue system.

Their “home” is the bright ecological city of Shtorm-Harbor.
Their “work” is the harsh fortress-city of Thundertown.
Between them lies the sky, the storms, and the lives they protect.

Voxdergs have their own history, their own traits, and even their own legend. The most well-known of them is Johnny — the first one who survived.

This is an open species. You can create your own Voxderg as long as you respect the rules of this world.
