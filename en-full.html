Introduction

Voxdergs are kind and fluffy anthropomorphic dragons fused with outdoor warning sirens. Their voices warn the residents of two cities about approaching storms.

They live in a world after global disasters. Humans and dragons built a new order together, and at its center are the Voxdergs — not as soldiers or heroes, but as a reliable part of the rescue system.

Their “home” is the bright ecological city of Shtorm-Harbor.
Their “work” is the harsh city of Thundertown.
Between them lies the sky, the storms, and the lives they protect.

Voxdergs have their own history, their own traits, and even their own legend. The most well-known of them is Johnny — the first one who survived.

This is an open species. You can create your own Voxderg as long as you respect the rules of this world.

The Tale of Saving Humanity (Lore)

If you have the skills, you can rewrite the lore in a more beautiful way.

Part 1: Calamity

July 26, 2341.
A superstorm hits the Atlantic coast for the first time. Category 6 tornadoes and powerful tsunamis wipe out coastal cities. Within a few months, such storms become regular.

July 2342.
An energy crisis begins. Fuel reserves are running out. Local conflicts over resources start in several countries.

February 2343.
People notice a decline in the dragon population. Some suggest that it is caused by the collapse of the ecosystem.

April 2344.
Scientists from different countries begin sharing climate model data. The first signs appear that the storms are being intensified by emissions from thermal power plants.

May 2345.
The major powers sign a "Temporary Truce". Wars stop, but the world remains unstable.

Part 2: Hope

September 12, 2345.
A summit called the "Last Chance Summit" takes place. The leaders of the Hurricane States and the Thunderstorm Federation meet to decide the fate of humanity.
They sign three simple but clear documents: the "Eternal Peace Treaty", the "Agreement on Clean Energy Production", and "Project Voxdergs".
After that, they hear a quiet voice: "Wise decision, mortals". No one is nearby. At first they think it is a hallucination caused by fatigue, but later everyone starts to think otherwise.

Part 3: New World

All available resources are directed toward designing and building reactors and cities.

January 2346.
Construction of the "Protector-1" reactor begins. The coast of the Hurricane States is chosen as the location. It is later named Shtorm-Harbor. A city for workers and engineers is built together with the reactor.

March 2346.
Construction of the "Protector-2" reactor begins. The northern part of the Thunderstorm Federation is chosen as the location. It is later named Thundertown. For safety, the city is designed as a polar superfortress.

To warn people about storms, scientists remember the old technology of sirens. They decide to take the sirens off their poles and make them alive.

Part 4: "Palladium" Experiments

October 2346.
The "Palladium" laboratory is founded. Its goal is to turn living beings into a warning system. After successful tests on mannequins, the scientists decide to look for living dragons.

March 2347 – March 2348.
The scientists make 44 attempts. They experiment with implant configurations, wiring, and new components. These experiments are cruel — none of the 44 dragons survive. Each dead dragon is named after a chemical element according to its atomic number. (Periodic Table)

May 15, 2348.
Attempt No. 45. This time a dragon comes to the laboratory on his own. It becomes the first successful experiment. The implants work properly. They name him Johnny and also give him the secret name Palladium-45.

June – September 2348.
Attempts No. 46–51. All of them are successful. The decision is made to begin serial production.

October 2348.
The “Palladium” laboratory is officially closed. Johnny and the other survivors are allowed to visit the laboratory. Engineers develop a way to create a Voxderg “from scratch.”

Part 5: The First Voxderg

January 11, 2349.
A siren sounds over Shtorm-Harbor. It is Module — a newly completed Voxderg who has just opened her eyes. A new naming system is introduced: the name of a regular siren model plus the next available number. Module becomes “Modulator-1” and is also given the final secret name “Tellurium-52.”

February – December 2349.
Serial production of Voxdergs begins. Module is found to have a configuration bug that allows her to sense Johnny from a distance.

March 2350.
Construction of Shtorm-Harbor is completed. The "Protector-1" reactor starts operating in the ecological city.

June 2350.
Construction of Thundertown is completed. The "Protector-2" reactor starts operating in the polar superfortress.

January 2351.
A proposal to build the "Protector-3" reactor in Belobyl, in the center of the Abnormal Republic, is accepted. Construction begins, but many people feel uneasy.

October 2352.
Construction of the reactor is stopped because of anomalies. Johnny decides to destroy the reactor.

November 2352.
Belobyl is declared an exclusion zone. People and furries leave the city.

Part 6: Present Day

2353 – 2356.
An era of stability begins. Everyone adapts to the new world. Voxdergs establish a “Home–Work” system between Shtorm-Harbor and Thundertown. New professions and hobbies appear, including stalking. The cities begin to flourish.

April 6, 2356.
The present day.

Everything is fine. And it will be fine.
